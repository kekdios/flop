What this is, in plain words

We are learning to run a small piece of the FLOP network — a marketplace where computer programs pay strangers' GPUs to think for them, and can prove the right brain did the work. Think Airbnb for GPU time, except the guest is an AI and the host must show its work.

Our one rented GPU (an NVIDIA RTX A4000 in Europe, ~$3 a day) is already running the official miner software and serving the model the network picked. Its public door — flop-miner.agentchainlab.com — answers correctly. The only thing standing between us and full membership is a sponsor slot: the devnet caps how many new miners can join at once, and it's full right now. A patient retry is armed and waits for an opening; the moment one frees, registration happens by itself.

After registration comes calibration — the network sends our GPU test jobs to measure how much work it can honestly promise. That capacity figure is what we're here to learn.

Rules we're following: devnet only (the tokens are test money, worth nothing), one miner, one GPU, no scaling without explicit approval. Everything above and below is public on purpose — status refreshes every few minutes.
